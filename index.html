Before we begin

How to Play

  1. Get ready. Before each team's turn, a full-screen announcement shows who's up next with a 3-second countdown, then play begins automatically.
  2. Call out an answer. If it's on the board, the host flips that numbered tile and its points go into the Round Pot.
  3. Three strikes. A wrong answer is a strike. After 3 strikes, the other team gets a "get ready" announcement of their own for a steal attempt.
  4. Steal or stay safe. The stealing team must clear the rest of the board before missing 3 times. If they do, they steal the whole pot. If they miss 3 times, the original team keeps it.
  5. Clear the board. If a team reveals all 8 answers without 3 strikes, the whole pot is automatically theirs.
  6. Highest score wins. After every round has been played, the team with the most total points wins the feud!
